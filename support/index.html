Common questions

Which Macs does Framelet Studio run on?

macOS 26 Tahoe or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. Every feature works on both. Apple silicon is recommended: on-device analysis is much faster on the Neural Engine.

Do I need to import my photos?

No. Framelet Studio works directly with your folders. Add as many as you like: their photos and videos appear together, with each folder one click away in the sidebar.

What are the keyboard shortcuts for cleaning up?

Press Space to preview any photo or play any video, f to add to favorites, d to mark for deletion, or Shift + D to delete immediately (Framelet Studio asks you to confirm first).

Does Framelet Studio delete my photos?

Never without asking. Items you mark wait in To Delete and go to the Trash together, only when you confirm. Undo works for every decision. Enhance saves a new copy unless you choose to replace the original, which also goes to the Trash.

Do favorites and labels show up in Finder?

Yes. Favorites and labels are saved as Finder tags on your files, so they show up in Finder too.

How do I save a still from a video?

Framelet Studio suggests a video’s featured frames, or you can pick any frame yourself, and saves them as photos.

Is finding duplicates free?

Yes. Framelet Studio finds exact copies, nearly identical shots and similar photos, suggests the best one to keep and marks the extra copies for you. No purchase needed.

What does Framelet Pro include?

Framelet Intelligence, as a one-time purchase:

  • Search by content: describe a photo, like “dog at the beach”, and Framelet Studio finds it. It reads scenes, objects and text.
  • Categories: People, Animals, Food & Drink, Landscapes, Documents and more.
  • Enhance Photo: one-click fixes and looks picked for each photo, for light, color and a level horizon, plus holiday looks (Christmas, Halloween, New Year, Lunar New Year, Valentine’s, Diwali and more) and cartoon stickers like a Santa hat, pumpkin or party hat.
  • Extract Subject: lift the subject off its background and copy it, or save it as a PNG or a sticker with an outline, glow or comic style.

Search by content doesn’t find my photo.

Search by content understands English descriptions. Try describing what’s in the photo in English, such as “birthday cake” or “mountain lake”.

I bought Framelet Pro. How do I get it on another Mac?

Sign in to the Mac App Store with the same Apple Account and open Framelet Studio; Pro unlocks on its own. If it doesn’t, choose Settings ▸ About ▸ Restore Purchases.

Framelet Studio can’t open a folder it opened before.

The folder may have been moved, renamed or be on a disk that isn’t connected. Framelet Studio marks it in the sidebar; add it again with File ▸ Add Folder… to give Framelet Studio access.

How do I free the space Framelet Studio uses?

Framelet Studio keeps thumbnail and analysis caches on your Mac to stay fast. Settings ▸ Storage shows them and lets you clear them at any time.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. All analysis runs on your Mac: on the Neural Engine on Apple silicon, and on the CPU and GPU on Intel. Framelet Studio collects no data, has no accounts and never uploads your photos. See the Privacy Policy.
