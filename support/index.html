Common questions

Which Macs does Framelet run on?

macOS 15 Sequoia or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. Apple silicon is recommended: on-device analysis is much faster on the Neural Engine.

I bought Framelet Pro. How do I get it on another Mac?

Sign in to the Mac App Store with the same Apple Account and open Framelet; Pro unlocks on its own. If it doesn’t, choose Settings ▸ About ▸ Restore Purchases.

What does Framelet Pro include?

Framelet Intelligence: search by content, Categories, Enhance Photo (with holiday looks and stickers) and Extract Subject. It’s a one-time purchase.

Does Framelet delete my photos?

Never directly. Photos you mark for removal are moved to the Trash only after you confirm, and you can undo it. Enhance saves a new copy unless you choose to replace the original, which also goes to the Trash.

Framelet can’t open a folder it opened before.

The folder may have been moved, renamed or be on a disk that isn’t connected. Framelet marks it in the sidebar; add it again with File ▸ Add Folder… to give Framelet access.

How do I free the space Framelet uses?

Framelet keeps thumbnail and analysis caches on your Mac to stay fast. Settings ▸ Storage shows them and lets you clear them at any time.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Framelet collects no data and never uploads your photos. See the Privacy Policy.
